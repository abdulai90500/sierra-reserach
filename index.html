Institutional Profile

About SiRI-Hub

Pioneering evidence-based digital skills, adolescent wellbeing, and systemic innovation across Africa.

A Pan-African Catalyst for Research & Digital Innovation

SiRI-Hub (Sustainable Innovation & Research Initiative Hub) is an independent, non-profit center of excellence dedicated to translating rigorous empirical research into high-impact digital education, youth empowerment, and public health interventions.

Our cross-disciplinary teams work at the intersection of pedagogy, technology, public health, and social policy to design evidence-based solutions that are community-owned and sustainable.

Evidence First

Every initiative stems from baseline data and field research.

Community Driven

Co-created with teachers, youth leaders, and caregivers.

Our History & Background

SiRI-Hub emerged from an urgent recognition among researchers, educational technologists, and community advocates that digital transformation in sub-Saharan Africa often lacked localized evidence and pedagogical support for front-line educators.

2021 — Genesis & Baseline Surveys

Field Diagnostics on Digital Skills Deficits

Initial assessment of 85 schools across peri-urban districts identified that hardware distribution failed without comprehensive educator training.

2023 — Launch of Tech4Teachers & Tech Clubs

From Theory to Field Implementation

Deployment of the first 25 school-based Digital Tech Clubs and accredited teacher development modules.

2025/2026 — Institutional Scaling

The Big Five Pillars & Regional Secretariat

Consolidation into SiRI-Hub with institutional governance, formal ministry advisory roles, and multi-country research partnerships.

🔭

Our Approach & Theory of Change (ToC)

How SiRI-Hub transforms empirical research and community co-creation into systemic educational and social impact.

Phase 01 • Diagnostics

Empirical Research & Needs Assessment

Rigorous field surveys, institutional readiness audits, and adolescent health baseline inquiries.

Phase 02 • Co-Creation

Intervention Design & Digital Tooling

Curriculum development for Tech4Teachers, peer-learning toolkits, and open-source lab architectures.

Phase 03 • Deployment

Community & Hub Implementation

Direct classroom training, youth digital tech clubs, and peer ASRHR dialog circles in hubs.

Phase 04 • Scaling

Policy Advocacy & Institutional Integration

Submitting policy briefs to education ministries and multilateral agencies to mainstream verified solutions.

Strategic Portfolios

The Big Five Pillars

A comprehensive overview of SiRI-Hub's five programmatic pillars, targets, and systemic interventions.

Pillar 01 Flagship Deployment: Tech4Teachers

Digital Education & Skills

We equip classroom teachers and school leaders with practical digital teaching competencies, pedagogical integration strategies, and offline open-source educational repositories that function reliably regardless of internet constraints.

Curriculum Integration

Transforming STEM lesson plans into interactive digital modules.

Offline Capability

Locally hosted content repositories eliminating connectivity barriers.

Educator Certification

Formal continuing professional development credits for educators.

Pillar 02 Community Care & Health Rights

Adolescent Sexual & Reproductive Health and Rights (ASRHR)

Providing age-appropriate, stigma-free sexual and reproductive health information, peer counselling, mental wellbeing support, and community advocacy that ensures youth make informed choices.

Peer Educator Networks

Trained adolescent champions leading youth-led health dialogues.

Community Caregiver Roundtables

Engaging parents, elders, and leaders to foster supportive environments.

Safe Referral Systems

Direct pathways to youth-friendly clinical services and psychosocial care.

Pillar 03 Scientific Rigor & Insights

Applied Research, Surveys & Policy Innovations

Publishing actionable research, longitudinal impact surveys, and technical evaluations that inform national curriculum boards, development partners, and community programs.

Longitudinal Surveys

Tracking learner performance and digital literacy trajectories over 3+ years.

Policy Synthesis

Translating academic findings into concise briefs for ministries.

Open Data Access

Curated anonymised datasets available for academic research fellows.
